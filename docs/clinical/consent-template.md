# Informed consent — structure (draft for the Institutional Ethics Committee)

To be completed by the site's ethics team in the local language(s). Items the software depends on are marked ★.

1. Study title, investigators, sponsor, contact.
2. Purpose: feasibility and safety of a head-mounted visual-reference aid for lateropulsion after stroke; **in addition to**, never instead of, standard therapy.
3. Procedures: registration, clinical scales, 60 s baseline, up to 5 sessions/week of ≤ 20 min headset time for 2–4 weeks; SSQ before and after sessions.
4. Risks: falls (mitigated by harness, supervision, gates), nausea/dizziness (stop rules, SSQ), skin contact (single-use liners), data privacy.
5. Benefits: none guaranteed; possible improved midline awareness.
6. ★ Data: what is recorded (head angle time series, session metrics, scale scores), where (encrypted on the device), how long (default 5 years), who can access, de-identified research export.
7. ★ **Separate consent item for video/screenshots** (`consent_media`), default off.
8. ★ Consent to de-identified research reuse after erasure (`consent_research`).
9. ★ Consent form version recorded with the patient record (`consent_version`, DPDP Act 2023 traceability).
10. Right to withdraw; erasure procedure; complaints contact; compensation for injury per site policy.
11. Signatures: participant / legally acceptable representative, witness, investigator; date.
